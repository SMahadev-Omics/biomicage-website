Who We Are

Expert Bioinformatics Team with Deep Real-World Omics Experience

BiOmicAge brings together a highly experienced team of bioinformaticians with hands-on expertise across the full spectrum of omics domains. Our capabilities span proteomics, lipidomics, genomics, metagenomics, single-cell analysis, spatial transcriptomics, cancer genomics, and integrated multi-omics workflows.

We go beyond executing pipelines — we interpret the underlying biology driving the data. Each project is led by domain specialists who have architected, implemented, and validated robust, reproducible pipelines in both research and clinical environments.

Our experience extends across academic research, precision health, and biotech applications. We deploy scalable analytics on cloud infrastructures such as Microsoft Azure and AWS, supporting everything from small-scale studies to large population-level cohorts.
