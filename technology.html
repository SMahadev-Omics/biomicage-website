Our Technology

The stack behind
every answer

Reproducible. Scalable. Cloud-native. AI-assisted. Deployable on any cloud platform. The BiOmicAge technology platform combines 50+ best-in-class bioinformatics tools with GPU-accelerated compute and enterprise-grade security — all on a single, customisable pipeline platform.

What Sets Us Apart

Six pillars of our platform

01
Fully Reproducible
Every run ships with version-locked conda environments, exact shell commands, and SHA-256 checksums on all inputs and outputs. Reviewers can reproduce results in under 30 seconds.
02
Cloud-Native & Scalable
Pipelines designed to run on Azure, AWS, or GCP. Containerised with Docker and Singularity. Scale from a single sample to population cohorts without changing a single line of code.
03
AI-Assisted
AI-driven QC, anomaly detection, and biological interpretation layer accelerates every stage of analysis — from automated flagging of outlier samples to AI-assisted report generation.
04
GPU-Accelerated
NVIDIA Parabricks powers our WGS pipeline with GPU acceleration — completing full genome analysis in under 5 hours, versus 30+ hours on conventional CPU-based approaches.
05
Highest Security
Data is handled in encrypted, access-controlled cloud environments with no unnecessary data movement. We follow best practices for genomic data privacy and security at every step.
06
Fully Customisable
Every pipeline parameter is configurable. Swap tools, adjust thresholds, add custom steps. Our platform supports bespoke pipeline design for any omics question or data type.
